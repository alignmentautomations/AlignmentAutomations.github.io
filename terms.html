2. What the service is

There are two plans. Starter Site is a one-time website build. The Done-for-You Intake System is a managed service that adds automated response and follow-up on top of it.

Starter Site

  • A conversion-focused, mobile-first website, 3–5 pages, built and hosted
  • Domain setup and configuration
  • Contact form and online booking
  • CSLB-compliant license and insurance display
  • An optional $49/mo care plan covering hosting, edits, and updates — never required

Done-for-You Intake System

Everything in Starter Site, plus:

  • Missed-call text-back with two-way SMS
  • Instant confirmation and automatic follow-up emails
  • Lead logging and notifications to you
  • Ongoing monitoring, edits, and maintenance

The software and tool costs required to run the Intake System are included in the monthly price. You do not need to buy, set up, or manage separate accounts for it. Missed-call text-back requires an EIN and a business (non-cell) phone line, plus a 10–15 business day carrier registration before it goes live — see Section 6.

Both plans include our Google Business Profile Setup Guide: a step-by-step walkthrough so you can claim and complete your own listing in about an hour. You keep full ownership and control of your profile.

4. The 30-day guarantee

This applies to the Done-for-You Intake System. If your system is not catching leads within the first 30 days after launch, tell me and I will stop the monthly charge. You keep the website and your domain.

To be straight with you about what this is and is not: the setup fee is not refunded, because the build work is already done, and you keep the result of it either way. What the guarantee removes is the risk of being locked into paying monthly for something that is not working for you.

Starter Site has no monthly charge to guarantee against — it is a one-time build governed by the revision terms in Section 3a instead.

5. Cancellation, and what you own

There is no long-term contract and no cancellation fee. Cancel any time by emailing me.

When you cancel, I hand over your domain, your website, your calendar setup, and your lead data. It is yours. I run it so you do not have to, but I do not hold it hostage to keep you as a client.

Handover takes a few business days, since some transfers depend on registrars and third-party services that set their own timelines.

6. Timelines

Typical timelines, once you have provided what I need:

  • Starter Site: 5–7 business days from kickoff to live, including your one round of revisions.
  • Intake System: about one week from kickoff to your site going live, including your review.
  • Intake System, about one more week before missed-call text-back switches on.

That last step waits on the phone carriers approving your business to send text messages, which is a required step for every business in the United States. That approval is not mine to grant or to rush, and it occasionally takes longer than a week. Everything else in your system works while it is pending. Starter Site has no carrier step — no EIN, no registration, no waiting period.

These are estimates based on normal conditions, not contractual deadlines. If something is going to run long, I will tell you rather than let you wonder.

The service is done for you, but it is not done without you. For either plan, I need:

Realistically this costs you about an hour of your time in total. If I cannot get what I need, the timeline pauses rather than the work getting done wrong.

9. What I do not promise

I want this section to be honest rather than lawyerly, because overpromising is how this industry earns its reputation.

I can promise that the system will be built, that it will work, and that I will keep it running. I cannot promise a specific number of leads, a specific revenue figure, or a specific return on your investment. Those depend on your market, your pricing, your reputation, your follow-through on the leads it catches, and plenty of other things outside a website.

What the system does is make sure the people already trying to reach you actually get through, and get an answer fast. That is a real and measurable improvement for most contractors. It is not a promise of growth, and anyone who does promise you that number is guessing.
