Privacy Policy

What I collect, why I collect it, who it goes to, and what you can ask me to do with it. Written in plain English, because a policy nobody can read protects nobody.

Last updated: August 14, 2026

The short version. I collect the contact details you type into a form, plus your IP address to stop bots from flooding those forms. I do not run analytics, advertising pixels, or cross-site trackers, and I do not sell your information to anyone. If you want your data deleted, email me and I will delete it.

Who I am

Alignment Automations is operated by Matthew Perry as a sole proprietorship, based in San Luis Obispo County, California. Throughout this policy, "I" and "me" mean Alignment Automations, and "you" means whoever is using the site.

Questions about anything here go to hello@alignmentautomations.com.

What I collect

Information you give me

When you submit a form on this site (booking a demo, requesting the intake audit, using the missed-call calculator, or emailing sales), I collect what you type:

  • Your first and last name
  • Your business name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your trade or service type, and your website, when you provide them
  • Anything you write in a message or notes field

If you book a call, the booking tool also records the time you picked and any details you enter there.

Information collected automatically

When you submit a form, I record the IP address the submission came from, along with a timestamp. This exists for one reason: to rate limit the form so that a bot cannot flood it, and to cap how many automatic replies go out in a day. These records are deleted after seven days.

My hosting and network providers (listed below) also keep their own standard server logs, which is normal for any website.

What I do not collect

This is worth stating plainly, because most sites cannot say it:

  • No analytics. There is no Google Analytics, no Cloudflare Web Analytics, and no equivalent on this site.
  • No advertising pixels. No Meta pixel, no Google Ads tag, no conversion tracking.
  • No cross-site tracking. I do not follow you around the internet, and I do not build an advertising profile on you.
  • No sale of personal information. I do not sell your data, and I do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Why I use it

  • To reply to you, which is usually the entire point of you filling out the form
  • To send the automatic confirmation and follow-up emails you triggered by contacting me
  • To prepare and deliver anything you asked for, such as an intake audit report
  • To keep a record of our conversation so I am not asking you the same questions twice
  • To bill you, if you become a client
  • To protect the forms from spam and abuse

I do not use your information for anything unrelated to the reason you gave it to me.

Beyond those services, I share your information only if you ask me to, or if I am legally required to, such as by a valid subpoena.

Text messages

I do not send marketing text messages to people who contact me through this site. If you give me your phone number here, I use it to call or text you about the specific thing you contacted me about, and nothing else.

Separately, the intake systems I build and operate for client businesses include an automatic text reply when a customer calls that business and nobody picks up. If you are that customer, here is how it works:

  • You consent by contacting the business. Calling a business and getting a text back is the entire service, and no other messages are sent to you.
  • Message and data rates may apply, depending on your carrier and plan.
  • Message frequency depends on how often you contact the business.
  • Reply STOP to any message to opt out. Reply HELP for help.
  • Opting out is honored immediately and permanently for that business.
  • Phone numbers and consent collected this way are never sold, and never shared with third parties for their own marketing.

Cookies

This site does not set advertising or analytics cookies. Cloudflare may set a strictly necessary cookie to keep the site secure and available. If you book through the scheduling tool, that tool may set its own cookies, governed by its own privacy policy.

How long I keep it

  • Form rate-limiting records, including IP addresses: deleted after seven days.
  • Contact and lead records: kept while there is an active or possible working relationship, and deleted on request.
  • Client records: kept for as long as you are a client, and afterward only as long as needed for tax and accounting obligations.

Your rights in California

California residents have rights under the CCPA and CPRA, and I extend the same handling to everyone who contacts me, regardless of where you live. You can ask me to:

  • Tell you what personal information I hold about you
  • Delete the personal information I hold about you
  • Correct anything that is wrong
  • Opt out of sale or sharing. There is nothing to opt out of, because I do not sell or share personal information for advertising, but the right exists and I am naming it.

I will not treat you differently for exercising any of these rights. To make a request, email hello@alignmentautomations.com from the address you contacted me with, or tell me enough that I can find your record. I aim to respond within 45 days, which is the deadline California law sets.

Children

This site is for business owners. It is not directed at anyone under 16, and I do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given me information, email me and I will delete it.

Security

Form submissions travel over an encrypted connection and are stored with the providers listed above. The forms are protected by an origin check, a spam trap, and rate limiting. No system is perfectly secure, and I will not pretend otherwise, but I do not collect sensitive categories of data such as government identifiers or health records, which limits what is at risk.

Changes to this policy

If I change this policy, I will update the date at the top. If a change is significant, such as starting to use analytics or sending marketing texts, I will say so clearly rather than quietly editing this page.
