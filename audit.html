The audit

Four tests, run on your actual business.

Nothing here is advice or opinion. It's a record of what happened when a real customer tried to give you money.

TEST 01

I call you during work hours

A normal customer call about a normal job. I note whether anyone picks up, how many rings it takes, and what happens when nobody does. Most crews are on a ladder or under a sink, which is exactly the point.

TEST 02

I leave a voicemail and start a clock

A real message, a real callback number, and a timestamp. Then I wait. That number, the hours between your customer calling and anybody calling back, is usually the single most expensive number in the report.

TEST 03

I fill out your own contact form

If you have one, I submit it and time the reply. I also check whether it went anywhere at all. Broken forms that quietly drop every submission are more common than you'd think, and the owner is always the last to know.

TEST 04

I put a dollar figure on every leak

Using your average job value and the number of leads you already get, each gap turns into a yearly cost. That's the difference between "you should answer faster" and "this is costing you eleven grand a year."
