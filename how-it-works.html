What happens next

The moment a lead comes in — here's what runs.

Every lead has two sides: what your customer experiences, and what happens on your end. You handle none of it.

📱 What your customer sees
  • An instant text and email reply — within seconds, not hours
  • A friendly confirmation that their request came through
  • A booking link to lock in an estimate at a time that works
  • A reminder before the appointment so they actually show
🔔 What happens on your end
  • An instant notification with their name, number, and what they need
  • The lead logged automatically — nothing to write down
  • The booking dropped onto your calendar, synced
  • No-shows and follow-ups handled for you
What you do: nothing. It all runs on its own while you stay on the job.
