Pricing Questions

What's included in the monthly fee?

Everything — hosting, the missed-call text-back, automatic follow-ups, online booking, lead logging, and the software it all runs on, plus ongoing monitoring and edits. One price, no separate bills.

Are there any tool or software costs on top of this?

No. The software your system runs on is included in the monthly price. You get one bill from me and never have to set up, pay for, or manage any accounts yourself.

What does the $1,500 setup cover?

The one-time setup covers building and connecting your whole system — domain, website, missed-call text-back, follow-up automations, booking, and lead logging — all configured and tested before it goes live.

I already have a website. Does the price change?

No — the plan is for the whole intake system, not just the web page. If your current site can support it, I'll build around it; if it can't, I'll rebuild it at no extra charge. Either way you get the same connected system for the same simple price.

Can I just get part of it?

The plan is built to run as one connected system — the website, instant response, follow-up, and booking work because they're wired together. That's also what keeps it simple and completely hands-off for you. If you already have a piece, like a website, I build around it at no extra charge. Got a situation that doesn't fit the box? Book a call and we'll talk it through.

What if it doesn't work?

Then you shouldn't keep paying for it. If your system isn't catching leads in the first 30 days, tell me and I'll stop the monthly — and you keep the website and your domain. The setup work is already done and it's yours either way.

What if I want to cancel?

Cancel anytime — there's no lock-in. I'll hand over your forms, calendar, data, and website whenever you want. Until then, I run all of it for you.

I'm not ready for the whole system yet. Is there a smaller step?

Yes. For $97 I'll run an intake audit on your business: I call you as a customer, leave a voicemail, fill out your contact form, and time every response. You get a written report showing exactly where leads are getting lost and what it costs you per year. No commitment to anything after it.

How long does setup take?

About two weeks from the kickoff call to going live. I build in the first week, you review a live preview, and it launches once you've approved it. Missed-call text-back switches on within about a week after that, once the phone carriers approve your business to send texts.
