Pricing Questions

What's the difference between the two plans?

Starter Site is a real, mobile-first website with a contact form and online booking — a one-time build, no monthly required. The Done-for-You Intake System adds the parts that answer for you when you can't: missed-call text-back, automatic follow-up sequences, and lead logging, fully managed for $297/mo. Starter gets you online fast; the Intake System runs the whole front door of your business.

Do I need an EIN?

Only for the Intake System. Missed-call text-back runs on a business phone line registered with the carriers, which requires an EIN and a business (non-cell) number, plus a 10–15 business day approval window. Starter Site needs none of that — no EIN, no carrier registration, no waiting period.

Can I upgrade from Starter to the Intake System later?

Yes. Starter's website becomes the foundation — I add missed-call text-back, follow-ups, and lead logging on top of it rather than rebuilding from scratch. You only pay the difference in setup, not for the site again.

What about my Google listing?

Both plans include our Google Business Profile Setup Guide — a step-by-step walkthrough so you can claim and complete your listing yourself, in about an hour. You keep full ownership and control of your own profile.

What's included in the Intake System's monthly fee?

Everything — hosting, the missed-call text-back, automatic follow-ups, online booking, lead logging, and the software it all runs on, plus ongoing monitoring and edits. One price, no separate bills.

Are there any tool or software costs on top of this?

No, on either plan. Starter's optional $49/mo care plan and the Intake System's $297/mo both include whatever software runs underneath. You never set up, pay for, or manage a separate account yourself.

What does the $1,500 Intake System setup cover?

The one-time setup covers building and connecting your whole system — domain, website, missed-call text-back, follow-up automations, booking, and lead logging — all configured and tested before it goes live.

I already have a website. What's my price?

Depends which plan. For the Intake System, the price doesn't change — if your current site can support it, I build around it; if it can't, I rebuild it at no extra charge, either way for the same $1,500 + $297/mo. Starter Site is priced as a new build, so it's a better fit if you're starting from nothing.

What if the Intake System doesn't work?

Then you shouldn't keep paying for it. If your system isn't answering and following up on your leads in the first 30 days, tell me and I'll stop the monthly — and you keep the website and your domain. The setup work is already done and it's yours either way.

What if I want to cancel the Intake System?

Cancel anytime — there's no lock-in. I'll hand over your forms, calendar, data, and website whenever you want. Until then, I run all of it for you.

I'm not ready for either plan yet. Is there a smaller step?

Yes. For $97 I'll run an intake audit on your business: I call you as a customer, leave a voicemail, fill out your contact form, and time every response. You get a written report showing exactly where leads are getting lost and what it costs you per year. No commitment to anything after it.

How long does each one take?

Starter Site: 5–7 days from kickoff to live. The Intake System: about two weeks from kickoff call to going live — I build in the first week, you review a live preview, and it launches once you've approved it. Missed-call text-back switches on within about a week after that, once the phone carriers approve your business to send texts.
