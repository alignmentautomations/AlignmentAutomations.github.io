# Google Business Profile Setup Guide — Outline

Bonus item included with both Starter Site and the Done-for-You Intake System.
The client completes this themselves; it is not a service Alignment Automations
performs. This file is an outline for the guide content — the guide itself
(PDF or page) is not built yet.

## 1. Claiming your profile
- How to tell if a profile already exists for the business (search Google Maps
  and Google Search directly, not just "Google Business Profile Manager").
- Claiming an existing, unclaimed listing.
- Creating a new listing from scratch if none exists.

## 2. Verification
- **Video verification is the default path now** — walk through what Google
  asks for and how to film it (showing the business name, address match,
  signage, equipment/vehicles).
- Where people get stuck: mismatched address, no visible signage for a
  service-area business, filming in a location that doesn't match what's on
  the listing.
- Postcard and phone verification as fallbacks, and when Google offers them
  instead of video.

## 3. Service-area business setup
- How to set up a service-area business (no walk-in customers) correctly.
- **Hiding the home/office address** — the specific toggle and why most
  contractors need this on.
- Setting the actual service area (city/zip radius) accurately.

## 4. Categories
- Choosing the right **primary category** — this is the single highest-impact
  decision on the whole profile.
- Adding relevant **secondary categories** without diluting the primary one.
- Common mistakes: too many categories, wrong primary category, categories
  that don't match what the business actually does.

## 5. Services list
- Writing out the **full services list**, not just a couple of examples.
- Matching service names to what customers actually search for.
- Adding descriptions to each service where Google allows it.

## 6. What's out of scope for this guide
- Ongoing review management, posts, or photo uploads — this is a one-time
  setup walkthrough, not an ongoing service.
