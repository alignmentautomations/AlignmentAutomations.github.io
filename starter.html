Starter Site Questions

Do I need an EIN or carrier registration for this?

No. Those requirements are specific to missed-call text-back on the Done-for-You Intake System. Starter Site is just a website — no EIN, no carrier registration, no waiting period.

What about my Google listing?

Starter Site includes our Google Business Profile Setup Guide — a step-by-step walkthrough so you can claim and complete your own listing in about an hour. You keep full ownership and control of your profile.

Can I upgrade to the Intake System later?

Yes. Your Starter site becomes the foundation — I add missed-call text-back, follow-ups, and lead logging on top of it rather than rebuilding from scratch. You only pay the difference in setup.

Is the $49/mo care plan required?

No, never. It's there if you want me to keep handling hosting, edits, and updates. If you skip it, the site is still yours, live, and working — you'd just make any future changes yourself or find your own developer.
